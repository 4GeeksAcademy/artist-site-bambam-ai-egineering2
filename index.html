About Me

Born between city noise and old vinyl records, Bamba developed a style that sits between modern electronic grooves and timeless vocal melodies.

Off stage, the focus is craft: writing sessions, collaboration, and pushing each track to feel cinematic while staying personal.

Career

From small local venues to festival side stages, the journey has been built on consistency, experimentation, and community support.
